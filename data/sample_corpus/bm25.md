# BM25 Sparse Retrieval

BM25 (Okapi BM25) is a sparse bag-of-words ranking function over inverted
indexes. Its score combines inverse document frequency with term frequency
saturation: the parameters k1 (typical 1.2, useful range 1.2 to 2.0) control
how quickly repeated terms stop adding score, and b (typical 0.75) controls
document-length normalization. Setting b to 0 disables length normalization;
setting it to 1 fully penalizes long documents.

BM25 excels at exact keyword matches: acronyms, error codes, function names,
version strings. Its weakness is vocabulary mismatch: it cannot connect
"KV cache" to "key-value memory" without shared tokens. That is why hybrid
retrieval pairs BM25 with dense embeddings, which capture paraphrase but
often miss exact identifiers.
